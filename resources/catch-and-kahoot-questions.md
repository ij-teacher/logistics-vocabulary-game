## Kahoot 四選一題目與答案

以下是題目稿，尚未建立或發布 Kahoot。每題一個正確答案；所有選項均取自保留給 Kahoot 的詞彙。

### 1. 「產地證明」的英文是什麼？

A. Certificate of Origin
B. Arrival Notice
C. Vendor / Supplier
D. Warehouse

答案：A — Certificate of Origin。A document showing where goods were made.

### 2. 「到貨通知」的英文是什麼？

A. Carrier
B. Arrival Notice
C. Release
D. Pallet

答案：B — Arrival Notice。A notice that tells you a shipment is arriving.

### 3. 「採購訂單」的英文是什麼？

A. Tariff / Duty
B. Warehouse
C. Purchase Order
D. Chargeable Weight

答案：C — Purchase Order。A document a buyer sends to order goods.

### 4. 「進口／出口」的英文是什麼？

A. Transit / Transshipment
B. Pallet
C. Arrival Notice
D. Import / Export

答案：D — Import / Export。Bring goods into a country / send goods to another country.

### 5. 「運送人」的英文是什麼？

A. Carrier
B. Consolidation
C. Chargeable Weight
D. Agent

答案：A — Carrier。A company that transports goods.

### 6. 「代理」的英文是什麼？

A. Carton
B. Agent
C. Arrival Notice
D. Inspection

答案：B — Agent。A person or company acting for another business.

### 7. 「供應商」的英文是什麼？

A. Storage / Demurrage
B. Carrier
C. Vendor / Supplier
D. Tracking / Status

答案：C — Vendor / Supplier。A person or company that supplies goods.

### 8. 「關稅／進出口稅」的英文是什麼？

A. Purchase Order
B. Inspection
C. Dangerous Goods
D. Tariff / Duty

答案：D — Tariff / Duty。A tariff is a tax or schedule of rates; duty is the tax charged on goods.

### 9. 「查驗」的英文是什麼？

A. Inspection
B. Agent
C. Tracking / Status
D. Volume

答案：A — Inspection。A check of goods or documents.

### 10. 「放行」的英文是什麼？

A. Inspection
B. Release
C. Dangerous Goods
D. Certificate of Origin

答案：B — Release。Permission to let goods go.

### 11. 「運送途中／轉運」的英文是什麼？

A. Warehouse
B. Volume
C. Transit / Transshipment
D. Import / Export

答案：C — Transit / Transshipment。Transit: goods travel through a place. Transshipment: goods change ships or vehicles.

### 12. 「貨物追蹤／狀態」的英文是什麼？

A. Pallet
B. Certificate of Origin
C. Vendor / Supplier
D. Tracking / Status

答案：D — Tracking / Status。Checking where goods are / information about their current stage.

### 13. 「倉庫」的英文是什麼？

A. Warehouse
B. Chargeable Weight
C. Import / Export
D. Release

答案：A — Warehouse。A building where goods are stored.

### 14. 「併裝／併櫃」的英文是什麼？

A. Arrival Notice
B. Consolidation
C. Vendor / Supplier
D. Warehouse

答案：B — Consolidation。Putting several small shipments together.

### 15. 「危險品」的英文是什麼？

A. Carrier
B. Release
C. Dangerous Goods
D. Carton

答案：C — Dangerous Goods。Goods that may cause harm during transport.

### 16. 「棧板」的英文是什麼？

A. Tariff / Duty
B. Warehouse
C. Storage / Demurrage
D. Pallet

答案：D — Pallet。A flat platform used to hold and move goods.

### 17. 「紙箱」的英文是什麼？

A. Carton
B. Transit / Transshipment
C. Pallet
D. Purchase Order

答案：A — Carton。A box made of strong cardboard.

### 18. 「體積／立方公尺」的英文是什麼？

A. Consolidation
B. Volume
C. Storage / Demurrage
D. Agent

答案：B — Volume。The space goods take up. CBM means cubic metre.

### 19. 「計費重量」的英文是什麼？

A. Carton
B. Purchase Order
C. Chargeable Weight
D. Inspection

答案：C — Chargeable Weight。The weight used to calculate the transport charge.

### 20. 「倉租／貨櫃逾期滯留費」的英文是什麼？

A. Certificate of Origin
B. Agent
C. Tracking / Status
D. Storage / Demurrage

答案：D — Storage / Demurrage。Storage: a fee for keeping goods. Demurrage: a container stays at a terminal beyond free time.
